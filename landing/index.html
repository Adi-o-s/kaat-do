The one rule

Every scam call needs one of three things from you.

Kaat Do teaches a single reflex, small enough to remember with a racing heart.

Darr · fear

Fear, or a hurry.

"Aaj raat aapka khata band ho jayega."

A ticking clock is how they stop you thinking.

Raaz · secrecy

A secret.

"Kisi ko mat batana. Phone mat kaatiye."

They keep you on the line so no one can talk you out of it.

Paisa · money

Money, an OTP, a code or an app.

"Woh chhe ank bataiye."

No bank, courier or police officer ever needs one on a call.
