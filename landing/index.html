Why a drill

Scammers rehearse their script. Our parents never get to rehearse theirs.

We send them warnings on WhatsApp. Then a calm, official-sounding voice calls, says the bank account is about to close, and the warning is forgotten. Knowing is not the same as reacting. A drill builds the reaction.

  • 1

    Real calls are designed to rush you

    Fear and a ticking clock leave no time to think, so the right move has to be a habit.

  • 2

    Practice is safe here

    Every call is fake. Nothing can be lost. A slip becomes a lesson, not a loss.

  • 3

    Advice lands better from family

    The coaching comes in your voice, so it sounds like you sitting beside them, not like a lecture.

How it works

Four steps. Two minutes from you, five from them.

You set it up once. Your parent just picks up the phone, the way they always do.

  1. 01You

    Record one sentence

    Read a short consent line aloud. Kaat Do checks that you really said it, then learns your voice for the coaching.

    "Yeh meri awaaz hai, aur is practice ke liye meri anumati hai..."
    Recording · 0:14
  2. 02Parent

    The phone rings

    Three practice calls in random order. Two are scams. One is a genuine courier, so they learn when not to cut too.

    It listens to every reply

    Your parent talks normally, in Hindi, English or both. Gnani Prisma turns each reply into text and Kaat Do decides how the "caller" responds.

    "Aap kaun? Main bank jaake poochungi."
    Heard as Pushed back
  4. 04Your voice

    A debrief from you

    A short voice note in your voice says what they did well and what to fix. Then it reveals that the voice was AI, and asks for a family code word.

    "Yeh awaaz AI thi." If a voice like mine ever asks for money, ask for our code word.

The one rule it teaches

Darr. Raaz. Paisa.Kaat do.

  • DarrFear or hurry. "Aaj raat khata band ho jayega."
  • RaazSecrecy. "Kisi ko mat batana."
  • PaisaMoney, an OTP, a code or an app. "Code bataiye."

If even one shows up, hang up. Then call family, or the cyber helpline 1930.

The result

A verdict, not a score.

Parents don't need a percentage. They need to know if they were safe, and the exact moment to fix.

  • PakkaCut every scam and kept the genuine call. Ready.
  • Bach gayeSafe in the end, but stayed on too long or wobbled once.
  • Phans gayeShared the code or agreed to pay. The debrief shows the exact line where it went wrong.

Every slip is shown with its proof: the words that were heard, or the OTP digits that matched. An unclear reply never counts against them.

Under the hood

Indian voices, understood the way Indians speak.

Parents switch between Hindi and English mid-sentence, on a noisy phone. Kaat Do is built on Gnani AI's speech models, made for exactly that.

1 · Caller speaks

A scripted line plays

Pre-recorded with Timbre at phone quality, so there is no lag.

2 · Parent replies

"Kaun bol raha hai?"

The browser records the reply and stops when they pause.

3 · Gnani Prisma

Speech to text

Hinglish reply to text in about a fifth of a second.

4 · Sorter

What did they do?

Agreed, pushed back, said they'd verify, read out the OTP, or hung up.

5 · Next line

The caller adapts

More pressure, the next red flag, or the call ends and the slip is noted.

Prisma

Gnani speech-to-text

The ears

Hears every reply the parent speaks, in Hindi, English or a mix.

  • One short request per turn, live during the call
  • Catches spoken OTP digits to spot a slip
  • Confirms you read the consent sentence before any cloning

Timbre

Gnani text-to-speech

The callers

Gives every fake caller a believable voice: the bank agent, the courier, the shouting "inspector".

  • Rendered at 8 kHz so it sounds like a real phone line
  • Audio tags for tone, like a shout or a sigh
  • Made once in advance, never during a call

Voice cloning

Gnani

The family voice

Speaks the debrief, and the optional fourth call, in your own voice.

  • Needs your live, checked consent first
  • Only speaks fixed lines from the script
  • Ends with the reveal: "this voice was AI"

Why it feels like a real call: nothing is generated while your parent is talking. The only live step is one quick transcription, so the "caller" answers without the pause that gives most AI calls away.

Built to be safe

A drill should never feel like a trap.

The calls are realistic on purpose, so the rules around them are strict.

Every screen says Practice

The amber "यह अभ्यास है" label never leaves the screen, from the first ring to the result.

The red button always works

Your parent can end any call at any second. No script can stop them.

No frightening stories

No accidents, kidnaps or hospital emergencies. The drill teaches, it does not shock.

Your voice, only with your consent

You read the consent sentence live. If it isn't heard, no voice is made. The cloned voice can't say anything outside the script.

Little is kept, briefly

Reply audio is thrown away after it is turned into text. Voice data and cloned clips are deleted after seven days.

Everything in it is made up

Every bank, courier, officer and number is fictional. The only OTP is a fake one shown on screen.

What it can't do yet: one drill doesn't make a habit, so repeat it every few months. It runs in a browser, not over a real phone line, so it reaches parents who use a smartphone.
